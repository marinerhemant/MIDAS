# PREREGISTER ADVICE (2026-10-28, fork VAL; fixed BEFORE any scenario below is run)

Module under test: `midas_sx.advice` (core `__init__.py`; checks `coverage.py` by fork COV, `quality.py` by fork QUAL). Their unit tests use synthetic stand-ins; this registration tests the module on realistic simulated FRAMES (independent simulator `validation/simulate.py`, `reduce_scan`, `workflow.merge_runs`, `advice.assess`) and on the real L-alanine reductions.

## Hypothesis
Each advice check fires, at the stated severity, when the defect it is meant to detect is injected into otherwise realistic frames, and the module raises no blocking or limiting finding on clean frames; on the real L-alanine data it reproduces the hand-measured numbers.

## Known-limits check
`~/.claude/known-limits.md`: no entry concerns acquisition advice. Entries on a free scale absorbing any dimming (KL on scale degeneracy) apply to the interpretation of intensity_drift, which is why the placebo guard of the check is tested (D8). Nothing else applies.

## Scene (all synthetic scenarios)
Structure `sim.structure_s2_bilayer()` (I4/mmm, 6 La + 4 Ni + 14 O), `sim.make_experiment(det_n=512, centre_rc=(255.5,255.5), omega_start=-38, n_frames=304, omega_step=0.25, dac_tilt_deg=(2,1), t_plus_mm=2.5, t_minus_mm=3.0, bg0=40, bg1=0)` (a 76 deg scan: the DAC opening is the limit), `sim.simulate(st, exp, orientation_seed=s, n_int_max=2e5, target_hits=40_000, max_pairs=8_000_000)`, frames `res.frames(seed=)`; header geometry 0.5 px and 1 % off as in tests/test_workflow.py; `reduce_scan(raw, crystal, U0, setup)` with U0 0.25 deg off; runs = orientation seeds 3, 4, 5 (C6 adds 6, 7, 8). Acquisition for all: `Acquisition(exposure_s=1.0, has_i0=False, has_calibrant=False, max_range_deg=76.0)` unless stated.
Defects are injected into the rendered SIGNAL before Poisson noise (frames = Poisson(signal * f + background)), not into the reduction.

## AMENDMENT 7 (2026-10-28, midas-17, after /verify of claim a0c60827daf1 returned 4 of 4 lenses UNCERTAIN; fixed BEFORE any re-run)

Both are MODULE defects, not scenario errors. The D2 and D3 passes in the READING above are withdrawn as detection evidence.
Repairs (rules fixed now):
1. `dynamic_range` gains a CLIPPING test. With a declared cutoff C: a blob is clipped when peak_counts >= 0.99 C. Without a cutoff: blobs whose peak equals the global maximum within 1e-3 relative; clipping needs at least 3 such blobs (a continuous peak distribution has a unique maximum). Measured: `blobs_clipped`, `observations_clipped`, `fraction_clipped` (of linked observations). Severity `limiting`, id `dynamic_range`, title naming "saturated", when fraction_clipped >= peak_obs_fraction (0.02, existing threshold, not retuned). Blobs with peak above C cannot exist on a real detector; if any do, the finding says the declared cutoff is inconsistent with the data (note). The existing count-rate-range note (25 % of C) stays as is and is NOT counted as detection of saturation.
2. `omega_sampling`: the undersampled rule is replaced. `omega_width_frames` is the intensity-weighted RMS of integer frame indices (midas_defect.ingest), NOT an extent; it is 0 for a blob on one frame. New measure `single_frame_fraction` = fraction of linked blobs with omega_width_frames == 0. New threshold `single_frame_fraction` = 0.25 (chosen now as "a quarter of the spots seen on one frame"; the lens measurements 0.508 in D3 and 0.013 in clean were seen before choosing, so it is a post hoc bar, stated). Undersampled note when the fraction is >= 0.25. The "median < 3 frames" criterion and the wording "upper estimate"/"extent" are removed (the RMS is not an upper bound); the median RMS stays reported as `median_rms_width_frames`. The oversampled rule (median > 30) is unchanged and untested.
