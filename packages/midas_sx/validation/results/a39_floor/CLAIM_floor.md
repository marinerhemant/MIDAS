## AMENDED WORDING after /verify (2026-10-23; all four lenses SURVIVES; amendments are the refuters' wording fixes)
- g: the data-estimated error-model g is 0.073 on the smooth-scaled data (0.182 on the run-scaled data, 0.200 on unscaled). The results are insensitive to g in 0.05-0.3 (R_F2 6.0-6.8 %). The sentence "g = 0.182" in the claim above belongs to the run-scaled data.
- The sentence "median Fo2/(k Fref2) ... baseline 0.85-0.88" compared two scale conventions. With the registered L1 scale the baseline shell medians are 0.92-1.01; the smooth + robust ones are 0.95-1.03. Dropped as evidence.
- The like-for-like placebo (permuted frames + robust merge) gives R_int 14.2 %; the robust merge alone (run scale + robust) gives 14.4 %. "The permuted-frame procedure does not lower R_int" holds for the smooth scale (default merge 15.3 %), not for the whole configuration. The two steps both contribute: R_F2 smooth + default merge 9.31 %, run scale + robust 11.33 %, both 6.33 %.
- "The reference is not used anywhere in the processing": the lattice, space group and the setting match of the reduction come from the same CIF file that holds the reference reflections (geometry only; no structure factors or reflection file are read before the merge). The polarization plane (0) was chosen on merged R_int; the reference was read at margins 1, 6 and 10 earlier (margin 6 kept although margin 10 gave a lower baseline R_F2); K = 8 was fixed before the claim-configuration reading; rel_floor 0.1 and the 4-sigma rejection were not provably frozen before the baseline reference reading (results insensitive: rel_floor 0.03-1.0 and reject 3-inf give R_int 6.6-7.1 %, R_F2 6.1-6.9 %).
- Where it does not help: the d < 0.8 A shell of R_F2 does not improve (6.2-5.7 % -> 6.8-7.1 %); the gain comes from d > 0.8 A. The 716 reflections are the stronger half of the reference reflections above our d_min (15-19 % of the weak half are seen). A single isotropic frame scale only partly captures an amplitude that grows with d (per-run frame-bin spread of ln(F2/Fref2) 0.13 at d < 0.8, 0.21 at d >= 1.1): a component that looks like extinction or path length remains.
- Stricter hold-outs (statistics lens): leave-frame-block-out error 19.6 -> 9.2 % (10 blocks per run), 22.8 -> 14.8 % (3 blocks); model-free cross-validation of R_F2 (scale fitted on one reflection half, scored on the other, 20 splits x 2): 13.8 -> 6.3 %; paired bootstrap of the R_F2 change -7.7 points, 95 % [-9.7, -5.4]. Flexible-scale null with no true drift and real noise structure: smooth scale lowers R_int by only 0.05-0.1 point; K = 8 to 48 knots flat at R_int 8.1 % (smooth + default merge).
- Mechanism: not established. The fitted scale follows an intensity variation that the independent reference also shows (log k per frame bin vs unscaled F2/Fref2: correlation 0.99); it is not an error in L, PT, tth or eta (those explain 1-2 % of the log ratio variance after the scale; frame bins explain 44-47 %). Absorption is excluded by magnitude; beam/crystal centring or illumination is the most plausible cause and untested.
- Scope: one compound, one crystal, three runs, one beamline.
